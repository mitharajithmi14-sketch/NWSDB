About NWSDB

The National Water Supply and Drainage Board provides safe drinking water and drainage services across Sri Lanka. This website lets every customer check bills and usage, and pay through more partners, without visiting a branch.

Services

Each online service is a separate .NET Web API behind one API gateway, so this website and partner apps use the same trusted data.

Account service

Register, log in and verify a water account.

Billing service

Current bill, due date and bill history.

Usage service

Meter readings and units used each month.

Payment service

Pay from the website or a partner and get a receipt number.

Notification service

Payment receipts by SMS or email, and customer messages.
